# ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Standard Mapping
## SDS-07 – IT Service Desk and Process Tracking System

**Project Code:** SDS-07  
**Document:** ISO_9001_Mapping.md  
**Version:** 1.1

## 1. Project Overview

The SDS-07 project aims to develop an IT Service Desk and Process Tracking System that manages corporate IT support requests according to ISO 9001 Quality Management principles.

The system covers Incident Management and Change Management processes. Its main objectives are to monitor Service Level Agreements (SLA), provide SLA violation warnings and escalation mechanisms, evaluate customer feedback and satisfaction surveys, and report process performance indicators (KPIs).

## 3. Application of ISO 9001 Clauses

### 3.1 Clause 8.2 – Requirements for Products and Services

This clause concerns communication with customers and determining service requirements.

**Application in SDS-07:**
- Users can create IT support requests.
- Requests can be classified as Incident or Change.
- Request descriptions and priority levels can be recorded.
- Response and resolution targets can be defined through SLA policies.

**Expected Result:** Clear definition and management of IT service requirements.

### 3.2 Clause 8.5.1 – Control of Service Provision

This clause concerns providing services under controlled conditions.

**Application in SDS-07:**
- Incident Management processes follow defined workflows.
- Support requests can be assigned to technicians.
- Request statuses are monitored throughout the process.
- SLA deadlines are tracked, and warnings or escalations are triggered when necessary.

**Expected Result:** Controlled and traceable IT support processes.

### 3.3 Clause 8.5.6 – Control of Changes

This clause concerns reviewing and controlling changes in service provision.

**Application in SDS-07:**
- Change requests are recorded in the system.
- The purpose and possible impact of changes can be evaluated.
- Approval steps and responsibilities can be tracked.
- Implementation results are documented.

**Expected Result:** Planned and controlled change management.

### 3.4 Clause 9.1.1 – Monitoring, Measurement, Analysis and Evaluation

This clause concerns measuring and evaluating process performance.

**Application in SDS-07:**
- SLA compliance rates are monitored.
- Average response and resolution times are measured.
- Open, resolved and delayed requests are reported.
- SLA violations and escalation numbers are analyzed.
- Performance indicators are displayed on the KPI Dashboard.

**Expected Result:** Measurable and reportable IT service performance.

### 3.5 Clause 9.1.2 – Customer Satisfaction

This clause concerns monitoring customer perceptions of service quality.

**Application in SDS-07:**
- Users can provide feedback after their support requests are resolved.
- Satisfaction surveys can be completed.
- Survey ratings and comments are recorded.
- Customer satisfaction results are evaluated periodically.

**Expected Result:** Continuous monitoring of customer satisfaction.

### 3.6 Clause 10.3 – Continual Improvement

This clause concerns continually improving the effectiveness of the quality management system.

**Application in SDS-07:**
- KPI reports are analyzed to identify performance problems.
- Repeated SLA violations are evaluated.
- Customer feedback is used to identify improvement opportunities.
- Improvement actions can be proposed for IT support processes.

**Expected Result:** Continuous improvement based on performance data and customer feedback.

### 3.7 Clause 7.5 – Documented Information

This clause concerns creating, updating and controlling documented information.

**Application in SDS-07:**
- Incident and Change records are maintained.
- Important status changes and system activities are recorded.
- SLA warnings and escalation activities can be tracked.
- KPI reports and satisfaction survey results can be stored.
- Project documentation is maintained using version control.

**Expected Result:** Traceable and accessible process documentation.

## 4. Common Project Terminology

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| Incident | An interruption or problem affecting an IT service |
| Change | A planned modification to an IT service or infrastructure |
| SLA | Service Level Agreement defining response and resolution targets |
| Escalation | Transferring a request to a higher-level team or responsible person |
| Customer Feedback | User comments about the received service |
| Satisfaction Survey | A survey used to measure customer satisfaction |
| KPI | Key Performance Indicator used to measure process performance |
| Dashboard | A visual panel displaying KPIs and process results |
| Audit Log | A record of important activities performed in the system |

## 5. General Process Flow

1. A user creates an IT support request.
2. The request is classified as Incident or Change.
3. The request is assigned to the responsible technician or team.
4. SLA targets are monitored.
5. Warnings and escalation mechanisms are triggered when required.
6. The completed process and its results are recorded.
7. Customer feedback and satisfaction surveys are collected.
8. KPI reports are analyzed to identify improvement opportunities.

## 6. Conclusion

The SDS-07 project is associated with ISO 9001:2015 clauses 8.2, 8.5.1, 8.5.6, 9.1.1, 9.1.2, 10.3 and 7.5.

These clauses support the project's objectives of managing IT service requirements, controlling incidents and changes, monitoring SLA performance, evaluating customer satisfaction, maintaining documented information and promoting continual improvement.

This document represents a project-level mapping for the analysis and design phase. It does not by itself demonstrate full ISO 9001 certification or compliance by an organization.
